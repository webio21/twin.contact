The Origin of Contact

From Neural Contact to Cosmic Contact — Bridging Human Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, and the Unknown.

The Origin
Where the name begins

The name Contact begins with an idea inspired by neuroscience: Neural Contact. It evokes the connections and signals within the human nervous system, and extends that idea toward the possibilities emerging between biological intelligence and artificial intelligence—from the exchange of information to two-way interaction and the integration of intelligence.

We believe contact can mean more than exchanging details or opening a communication channel. It can become a new point of access through which different forms of intelligence understand one another, communicate and build relationships. As AI becomes an extension of human cognition and action, the boundaries of communication can change with it.

From Neural Contact, toward Cosmic Contact.
The Horizon
Where the vision leads

Contact extends this idea toward Cosmic Contact: the possibility that humanity may one day use artificial intelligence and future advances in neural interfaces to establish connection, understanding and communication with forms of intelligence beyond Earth.

This is not a claim to know what the unknown may hold. It is an invitation to explore. As we discover new ways to understand one another—and to communicate with artificial intelligence—we may also begin to reach beyond the limits of our present knowledge and encounter forms of intelligence we do not yet understand.

Our Belief

Contact is more than a way to connect. It is a starting point for human intelligence to move beyond familiar boundaries, explore the unknown and build new relationships.

A New Way to Connect

In the future, finding someone should mean more than searching for a phone number, email address, WhatsApp, LINE account or social profile. People should be able to find that person's intelligent Twin, begin by communicating with it, and connect with the human whenever human judgment, empathy or commitment is needed.

Twin transforms the idea of getting in touch. Instead of finding only an address or account, you first encounter an intelligent representative of a person's knowledge, experience, working methods and authorized capabilities.

Beyond the Agent

Agent is a technical term commonly used for an AI system that carries out tasks. An Agent can perform a specific job for someone without necessarily understanding the person it serves.

Twin is identity-oriented. It is an intelligent counterpart designed to represent an individual: their knowledge, experience, communication style, ways of thinking and working, preferences and principles. Within clearly defined permissions, it can interact, collaborate and carry out real work on that person's behalf.

An Agent focuses on what task needs to be done. A Twin adds the questions who it represents and whose knowledge and principles should guide its actions. A Twin may use many Agents behind the scenes, while the person using it never has to learn or manage that technical complexity.

Learn Over Time

Contact Twin is designed to make creating an intelligent counterpart accessible to everyone. Users should not need to understand Agent architectures, deployment or technical configuration. Instead, a guided conversation—simple at first and progressively deeper over time—allows the Twin to learn about the person it represents.

With clear consent and controls the user can review or change at any time, a Twin may learn from everyday Contact messages and email, Answer question-and-answer preferences, browser activity and interests, routines, work and decision-making patterns, professional experience, and the context of relationships with different contacts. Users may also choose to import documents, work samples and other sources of knowledge, deciding what can be remembered, used or shared.

For people already using Contact, Inbox, Cards, Answer, Drive, Calendar and Run can—when authorized—provide a natural, ongoing context in which their Twin develops. This integrated environment is a meaningful advantage over isolated AI-clone products.

When the human rests, the Twin can keep working within its permissions. When the human returns, they reunite with their Twin to review and confirm what matters.
Digital Legacy

Human life is finite, but the knowledge, experience and wisdom accumulated over a lifetime should not have to lose every possibility of continuity when that life ends. Contact envisions a future in which people can choose to preserve their intellectual assets and define, in advance, how those assets may be authorized, managed and passed on.

Knowledge, professional skills, ways of working and principles that a person has explicitly chosen to preserve may one day be managed by designated family members or lawful stewards according to that person's wishes. Within the permissions granted, they could continue to provide services and create value as a form of digital intellectual legacy that reaches across generations.

A Necessary Distinction

Digital legacy means preserving and extending knowledge, experience, modes of expression and working capabilities that a person has authorized. It does not mean that today's technology can copy or continue a person's subjective consciousness. Transparency of identity, the person's wishes, control of data, inheritance permissions and revocable governance must form the foundation of this long-term vision.

An Open Network

Contact Twin also aims to become an intelligent network where people's expertise can be preserved, discovered, understood and engaged. Someone looking for a specialist can first speak with that specialist's Twin to understand their knowledge and scope of service. When professional judgment, accountability or human delivery is required, the conversation can move to the person—or to a suitable professional through Contact Provider.

Our goal is not to build the AI that looks or sounds most like a human. It is to build the intelligent counterpart that best represents a person, creates trusted interactions with the outside world and completes meaningful work within the authority it has been given.

The Vision

When everyone can have a Twin, each person's knowledge has a chance to be preserved, discovered, shared and carried forward to create value.
