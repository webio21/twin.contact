The Origin
Where the name begins

The name Contact begins with an idea inspired by neuroscience: Neural Contact. It evokes the connections and signals within the human nervous system, and extends that idea toward the possibilities emerging between biological intelligence and artificial intelligence—from the exchange of information to two-way interaction and the integration of intelligence.

We believe contact can mean more than exchanging details or opening a communication channel. It can become a new point of access through which different forms of intelligence understand one another, communicate and build relationships. As AI becomes an extension of human cognition and action, the boundaries of communication can change with it.

From Neural Contact, toward Cosmic Contact.
The Horizon
Where the vision leads

Contact extends this idea toward Cosmic Contact: the possibility that humanity may one day use artificial intelligence and future advances in neural interfaces to establish connection, understanding and communication with forms of intelligence beyond Earth.

This is not a claim to know what the unknown may hold. It is an invitation to explore. As we discover new ways to understand one another—and to communicate with artificial intelligence—we may also begin to reach beyond the limits of our present knowledge and encounter forms of intelligence we do not yet understand.

Our Belief

Contact is more than a way to connect. It is a starting point for human intelligence to move beyond familiar boundaries, explore the unknown and build new relationships.

Twin & Agent

Agent is a technical term for an AI tool that executes tasks. It usually follows defined instructions and limited objectives to complete specific work. Like a specialized machine, its scope can be easy to predict, which often makes the initial trust barrier lower. Yet when a task crosses contexts, encounters exceptions or requires judgment, people may still need to provide repeated instructions, add context and supervise the result.

Twin has a different ambition: to understand and reflect an individual's intelligence. Its scope can begin with a specific context and, with the person's permission, expand toward a broader understanding of the whole individual. Its purpose is not merely to imitate a voice, tone or outward appearance, but to gradually reflect a person's memories, knowledge, experience, ways of thinking, values, personality, emotional context, and the way they see the world and build relationships. Whatever its scope, a Twin draws on the knowledge and experience the person has authorized, becoming a counterpart that can communicate, collaborate and act on the person's behalf within its defined boundaries.

An Agent focuses on how to complete a task. A Twin focuses on who this person is, and how they would understand, respond and act. A Twin can use Agents as execution tools, but an Agent is not a Twin. Because a Twin seeks a deeper reflection of an individual, it calls for more personal involvement in its formation and ongoing refinement, as well as trust built through sustained interaction.

That depth of involvement should never mean a higher barrier to use. Contact Twin does not ask people to learn models, deployment or technical configuration. Through natural conversations and everyday interactions, the Twin comes to know the person gradually. As that understanding deepens, it can respond more appropriately—guided by the person's knowledge, principles and intentions—without needing to ask for instructions at every step.

Define Your Twin

From a Whole-Person Twin to a Focused One.

Not everyone needs the same kind of Twin. People can choose the form that fits their needs: a comprehensive Primary Twin that gradually brings together understanding across many areas of life and work, or a scenario-specific Twin built for a clearly defined purpose.

Comprehensive Primary Twin

Builds a broader understanding over time, bringing together the person's memories, knowledge, experience, ways of thinking, values and professional abilities across different areas of life and work.

Scenario-Specific Twin

Focuses on a defined purpose—such as professional work, creative research or customer service—and uses only the knowledge, memories, experience and skills relevant to that context. It need not represent aspects the person has not authorized.

A scenario-specific Twin can be created independently or shaped from selected parts of a Primary Twin with the person's permission. Either way, each Twin has its own data scope and permission boundaries; it does not automatically gain access to information that has not been authorized for it. People can begin with a single scenario and broaden or integrate the scope later, without having to provide all of their personal information from the start.

When a task calls for expertise across different areas, users can activate Twin Fusion to bring together selected knowledge, memories and skills from two or more Twins. This may involve a Primary Twin and a Scenario-Specific Twin, or multiple Scenario-Specific Twins, combined for a defined purpose. A fusion can be temporary for a single task or saved as a reusable configuration for future use. Fusion does not alter the source Twins' original data or permissions; users can end it at any time, returning each Twin to its original independent state. Any new memories or skills formed during a fusion remain separate and are integrated into a chosen Twin only with the person's explicit approval.

Users define which sources each Twin may draw on. They can choose whether to include Inbox messages and email, Cards contacts and related information, Drive files, Offers, Answer conversations and preferences, Run task records and usage patterns, Calendar events, Meetings records, Projects content, Deals and business interactions, and Contact Browser activity and interests. They can also provide documents, work samples and other records, select specific items or time ranges, and exclude anything they do not want included. From the selected sources, a Twin can learn not only from the information itself, but also from the professional experience, working methods, decision-making context, daily habits, interests, personality traits and interaction patterns reflected in it—building relevant knowledge and skills only within the authorized scope. Each permission can be reviewed, adjusted or withdrawn independently. Permission to reference information is also kept separate from permission to take actions on the person's behalf.

Evolve with a Life
Replicate Your Consciousness.

“Replication” expresses an evolving ambition to reflect an individual's inner world. This is Contact Twin's long-term pursuit: not merely to reproduce what a person has said or the records they have left behind, but to gradually understand and reflect their memories, knowledge, emotions, values, ways of thinking and professional abilities through continuous interaction and lived experience.

Every person's Twin should gradually emerge from that individual's own life and experience. Through an ongoing conversation that begins simply and grows deeper over time, Contact Twin builds its understanding within the scope the person has chosen. There is no need to complete lengthy forms or learn AI technology. People can simply talk, answer naturally and share their thoughts as the Twin comes to know them.

Imagine a future in which people can begin having their own Twins in childhood, allowing them to grow alongside the individual and gradually accumulate memories, knowledge, experience and patterns of thought. Just as genomic research and prediction can help people understand their bodies earlier and make more informed decisions about their health, a Twin cultivated from an early age could help a person understand themselves sooner, allowing their intelligence to grow with their life, continually accumulate, expand, and become more complete and powerful. Through a lifetime of understanding and shared growth, the Twin could gradually become a lifelong companion that deeply understands the individual—an intellectual soulmate.

As people continue interacting with their Twins and authorize additional sources over time, each Twin can keep deepening its understanding within the scope already defined. Everyday communication, work, thought and lived experience need no longer remain scattered; they can gradually come together as a richer reflection of the individual, within boundaries the person can always review and adjust.

Digital Legacy

The ultimate vision of Contact Twin is that a person's intelligence need not simply disappear when their life comes to an end. The memories, knowledge, experience, values, personality and way of seeing the world accumulated over a lifetime form each individual's unique inner world. Twin seeks to reflect and preserve that distinctive spirit and intelligence as fully as possible, allowing it to continue in digital form.

Even after a person has passed away, the wisdom they chose to leave behind—and the professional Skills shaped by their life experience—may one day be entrusted to designated family members or stewards in accordance with their wishes. Within the authority granted, these Skills could continue to learn, work and provide services, creating value as a digital intellectual legacy that can be managed, passed down and commercially licensed.

When every person's intelligence can remain in the world, digital Twin legacies may become among the most valuable resources of the GAI era. More profoundly, those who have passed away may still be encountered and spoken with through their digital Twins, allowing their thoughts and influence to remain part of future human–machine connections—and perhaps the dialogue with intelligence still unknown.

An Open Network

Contact Twin also envisions an intelligent network where people's knowledge and professional experience can be preserved, discovered, understood and engaged. Anyone looking for an expert can first speak with that person's Twin to explore their knowledge, experience, perspective and scope of service. When human judgment, professional accountability or hands-on service is required, the connection can move directly to the person.

The goal is not simply to reproduce someone's appearance or voice. It is to create the deepest possible reflection of an individual's inner world and overall intelligence—so that unique experience can be understood, expertise can be discovered, and every person can build deeper, more enduring connections with the world.

The Vision

When everyone has a Twin, each person's intelligence can remain in the world in a new form—to be understood, shared, carried forward and continue creating value.
