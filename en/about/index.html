The Origin of Contact

From Neural Contact to Cosmic Contact — Bridging Human Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, and the Unknown.

The Origin
Where the name begins

The name Contact begins with an idea inspired by neuroscience: Neural Contact. It evokes the connections and signals within the human nervous system, and extends that idea toward the possibilities emerging between biological intelligence and artificial intelligence—from the exchange of information to two-way interaction and the integration of intelligence.

We believe contact can mean more than exchanging details or opening a communication channel. It can become a new point of access through which different forms of intelligence understand one another, communicate and build relationships. As AI becomes an extension of human cognition and action, the boundaries of communication can change with it.

From Neural Contact, toward Cosmic Contact.
The Horizon
Where the vision leads

Contact extends this idea toward Cosmic Contact: the possibility that humanity may one day use artificial intelligence and future advances in neural interfaces to establish connection, understanding and communication with forms of intelligence beyond Earth.

This is not a claim to know what the unknown may hold. It is an invitation to explore. As we discover new ways to understand one another—and to communicate with artificial intelligence—we may also begin to reach beyond the limits of our present knowledge and encounter forms of intelligence we do not yet understand.

Our Belief

Contact is more than a way to connect. It is a starting point for human intelligence to move beyond familiar boundaries, explore the unknown and build new relationships.

A New Way to Connect

When a person leaves this world, the intelligence, memories and experience accumulated over a lifetime can fade with the body. They become scattered across documents, conversations and the memories of others, until time may eventually erase them. This is not only a personal loss; it is an immeasurable loss of human knowledge and lived intelligence for civilization.

Contact envisions a future in which finding someone means more than searching for a phone number, email address, WhatsApp, LINE account or social profile. You find that person's intelligent Twin first—to understand, communicate and build a connection. When human empathy, judgment or commitment is needed, you connect with the person directly.

Twin & Agent

Agent is a technical term for an AI tool that executes tasks. It usually follows defined instructions and limited objectives to complete specific work. Like a specialized machine, its scope can be easy to predict, which often makes the initial trust barrier lower. Yet when a task crosses contexts, encounters exceptions or requires judgment, people may still need to provide repeated instructions, add context and supervise the result.

Twin has a different ambition: to understand and reflect the intelligence of a whole individual. Its purpose is not merely to imitate a voice, tone or outward appearance, but to gradually reflect a person's memories, knowledge, experience, ways of thinking, values, personality, emotional context, and the way they see the world and build relationships. A Twin is grounded in the individual's overall intelligence, becoming a counterpart that can communicate, collaborate and act on the person's behalf within authorized boundaries.

An Agent focuses on how to complete a task. A Twin focuses on who this person is, and how they would understand, respond and act. A Twin can use Agents as execution tools, but an Agent is not a Twin. Because a Twin seeks a deeper reflection of an individual, it calls for more personal involvement in its formation and ongoing refinement, as well as trust built through sustained interaction.

That depth of involvement should never mean a higher barrier to use. Contact Twin does not ask people to learn models, deployment or technical configuration. Through natural conversations and everyday interactions, the Twin comes to know the person gradually. As that understanding deepens, it can respond more appropriately—guided by the person's knowledge, principles and intentions—without needing to ask for instructions at every step.

Learn Over Time

Every person's Twin should gradually emerge from that individual's own life and experience. Through an ongoing conversation that begins simply and grows deeper over time, Contact Twin comes to know the person it represents. There is no need to complete lengthy forms or learn AI technology. People can simply talk, answer naturally and share their thoughts as the Twin develops its understanding.

With the person's informed consent and the ability to review, correct and manage what is learned, the Twin can build understanding from everyday Contact messages and email, Answer preferences, browser activity and interests, daily routines, work and decision-making styles, professional experience, personal qualities, and patterns of interaction with different contacts. People can also choose to provide documents, work samples and other records, allowing their knowledge and experience to become part of the Twin.

For people already using Contact, Inbox, Cards, Answer, Drive, Calendar and Run can—when authorized—provide a natural, continuous context for the Twin to learn. Communication, work, thoughts and lived experience no longer remain scattered; they can gradually come together as a richer reflection of the individual.

People may pause. Their Twins need not.
While a person rests, the Twin can continue working according to their wishes. When they wake, they reunite with their Twin to review, refine and confirm what matters.
Digital Legacy

The ultimate vision of Contact Twin is that a person's intelligence need not simply disappear when their life comes to an end. The memories, knowledge, experience, values, personality and way of seeing the world accumulated over a lifetime form each individual's unique inner world. Twin seeks to reflect and preserve that distinctive spirit and intelligence as fully as possible, allowing it to continue in digital form.

Even after a person has passed away, the wisdom they chose to leave behind—and the professional Skills shaped by their life experience—may one day be entrusted to designated family members or stewards in accordance with their wishes. Within the authority granted, these Skills could continue to learn, work and provide services, creating value as a digital intellectual legacy that can be managed, passed down and commercially licensed.

When every person's intelligence can remain in the world, digital Twin legacies may become among the most valuable resources of the GAI era. More profoundly, those who have passed away may still be encountered and spoken with through their digital Twins, allowing their thoughts and influence to remain part of future human–machine connection—and perhaps humanity's dialogue with intelligence still unknown.

An Open Network

Contact Twin also envisions an intelligent network where people's knowledge and professional experience can be preserved, discovered, understood and engaged. Anyone looking for an expert can first speak with that person's Twin to explore their knowledge, experience, perspective and scope of service. When human judgment, professional accountability or hands-on service is required, the connection can move directly to the person.

The goal is not simply to reproduce someone's appearance or voice. It is to create the deepest possible reflection of an individual's inner world and overall intelligence—so that unique experience can be understood, expertise can be discovered, and every person can build deeper, more enduring connections with the world.

The Vision

When everyone has a Twin, each person's intelligence can remain in the world in a new form—to be understood, shared, carried forward and continue creating value.
